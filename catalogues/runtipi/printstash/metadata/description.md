# PrintStash

PrintStash is a self-hosted 3D print library and G-code vault. It keeps your
models, sliced revisions, printer copies and real print outcomes in one searchable
place on your own server.

- **One library for every file:** STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP/STP, G-code and BGCODE, with
  collections, tags, search, duplicate detection and in-browser previews.
- **G-code that works:** attach several slices to one model, compare slicer
  settings, and mark revisions as known good or failed.
- **Existing folders stay where they are:** index a NAS folder in place and keep
  it in sync.
- **Printers:** send prints and follow jobs on Klipper/Moonraker, with beta support
  for OctoPrint, PrusaLink, Bambu Lab (LAN) and Elegoo.

## First start

Runtipi creates the administrator from the username and password you enter when
installing. Sign in with them and PrintStash asks where to store your files;
the default keeps everything in the app's data folder.

The password in the install form is used once: editing it later does not change
the account. To change it, reset it under **Settings → Users** in PrintStash.

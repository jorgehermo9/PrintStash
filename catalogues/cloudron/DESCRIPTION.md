PrintStash keeps your 3D models, sliced G-code revisions and print history in one
searchable library on your own server.

- Preview STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP and G-code in the browser, with collections, tags,
  search and duplicate detection.
- Attach several slices to one model, compare slicer settings, and mark the
  revisions that printed well.
- Index an existing folder in place instead of copying it.
- Send prints and follow jobs on Klipper/Moonraker, with beta support for
  OctoPrint, PrusaLink, Bambu Lab (LAN) and Elegoo printers.

Everything lives in one data directory, so Cloudron's backup covers the database,
the library, thumbnails and local backups together. Updates keep it in place.
